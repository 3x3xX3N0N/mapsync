# Word -> SUMO term lexicon for the offline tag suggester (`suggest`): `word Term [weight]`.
# In the spirit of the WordNet->SUMO mappings (Niles & Pease), but hand-curated and tiny.
# Words are stemmed on load and in the text (lowercase; plural s/es, -ing, -ed stripped), so
# write them in their plain form. Weight defaults to 1. A word may name several terms.
# Every Term must be a base term (terms.txt) or a local term (local.txt).
# Pinned: its sha256 is a leaf under top_root (index.json lexicon_sha).
